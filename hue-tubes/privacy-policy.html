Hue Tubes – Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026

Overview

Hue Tubes is a puzzle game made by Hypertap Studios. You can play without creating an account. The game never asks for your name, email, contacts, photos or location. The game may show ads, offers optional in-app purchases, and uses Google services for analytics, crash reports, configuration and optional cloud saves, as described below.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, unlocked styles), your settings and a random player ID are saved on your device so you can continue where you left off. You can delete your progress at any time from Settings → Reset progress, or everything by uninstalling the app.

Advertising

The game may show ads provided by Google AdMob, for example a full-screen ad between levels or an optional video you choose to watch for a reward. To deliver, measure and prevent fraud in ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and ad interactions. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), we ask for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

In-app purchases

You can buy coin packs and Remove Ads through Google Play. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy; we never see your card or other payment details. To deliver what you bought, the app receives a purchase confirmation from Google Play (product, order ID, purchase token and time) and keeps the purchase tokens of coin packs on your device so each pack is delivered exactly once. Remove Ads is linked to your Google account and is restored from Google Play when you reinstall or use "Restore purchases". If "Share usage data" is on, which product you chose and whether the purchase completed, was cancelled or is pending are included in usage analytics, and Google Analytics for Firebase may record completed purchases (product, price and currency) for revenue reports. Payment methods are never shared with us.

Usage analytics and crash reports

If "Share usage data" is on (Settings → Privacy), the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played so we can balance levels, and Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports so we can fix bugs. This includes gameplay events (levels started, finished or restarted, moves, stars, time played, hints and other tools used, coins earned and spent, purchases started and their outcome, ads watched, screens viewed), crash details (stack traces, device model, operating system, app version, memory and disk state at the time of the crash), your random player ID, an app-instance identifier, language and approximate region. Analytics does not use the advertising ID. Turning the setting off stops this collection and resets the analytics identifier on your device.

Remote configuration

The app downloads settings from Firebase Remote Config (for example whether ads are shown and how often). This request uses a Firebase installation identifier and basic device and app information.

Google Play Games and cloud saves

If you use Google Play Games on your device, the game may sign you in automatically. We then use your Play Games player ID (a game-specific identifier, not your email) as your player ID so your statistics stay consistent, and we save a copy of your progress (levels, stars, coins, unlocked styles and daily puzzles) to Google Play Games Saved Games in your own Google account so it survives reinstalling or changing phones. Your Play Games name is shown only inside the app and is not sent to analytics. You can delete saved game data from the Play Games app.

How data is shared and kept

Analytics, crash and configuration data are processed by Google as our service provider. Ad data is collected by Google AdMob as described above. Payments are processed by Google Play. We never sell your data. Analytics event data is kept for no longer than 14 months and crash reports for 90 days, and is then deleted automatically.

Your choices and data deletion

You can turn analytics and crash reporting off at any time in Settings, change ad consent in Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices (where available), and reset your advertising ID in Android settings. To ask us to delete analytics and crash data linked to your player ID, email hypertapstudios.support@gmail.com with the Player ID shown in Settings → Data. We will delete it within 30 days.

Device backups

If you have enabled Android backup, your device may include the game's saved progress in your personal backup to your Google account. This is controlled by your device settings and handled by Google.

Permissions

The app uses internet access for ads, analytics, crash reports, configuration and Google Play Games, the Google Play billing permission for in-app purchases, the advertising ID permission for ads, and the vibration permission for optional haptic feedback. It does not request any runtime (dangerous) permissions.

Children's privacy

The game is not directed at children under 13, and ads are not targeted at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update it in the app and on our website and update the effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hypertapstudios.support@gmail.com.
